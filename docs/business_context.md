# Business Context

## Problem

An e-commerce marketplace generates fragmented operational data across orders,
customers, products, sellers, payments, reviews, and geolocation. Raw operational
data alone does not provide reliable business intelligence.

## Goal

Integrate, validate, standardise, and model that data into trustworthy analytical
datasets that answer marketplace questions such as revenue evolution, top
categories and sellers, delivery performance, payment behaviour, and the
relationship between delivery time and review scores.

## Users

- Data analysts and BI consumers querying Gold aggregates and the warehouse.
- Data engineers operating and extending the pipeline.
- Reviewers assessing engineering quality.

## Scope

- Batch processing of the Olist public dataset.
- Local-first execution with optional cloud mapping documented separately.
- No paid cloud resources are created.
